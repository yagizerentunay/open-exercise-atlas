## Coverage scan

The starting catalog had 37 exercises tagged with machine equipment, all of them already had a media asset. Among the named larger groups, machine-tagged coverage was: chest 7; upper back 4; lats 4; triceps 7; quadriceps 4; hamstrings 9. The scan found no missing machine images to backfill. These additions fill distinct movement-path gaps rather than duplicate existing record meanings: seated decline press, chest-supported low-angle row, and pendulum squat. Existing seated machine triceps extension and leg extension/curl/press records remain in place.

Forearms had no direct resistance-machine wrist flexion/extension record; direct wrist work is already represented by cable and free-weight records. That is a separate, lower-priority gap because a dedicated wrist machine is less common. This batch adds no neck or small-muscle priority movements.

## Artwork and sources

OpenAI ImageGen produced original transparent two-phase illustrations in the Atlas approved grayscale style with red target-muscle highlights. The generated PNGs were resized to 1024 × 1024 and encoded as transparent WebP at quality 85. No third-party artwork or instructions were copied.

- [Life Fitness Plate Loaded Decline Chest Press](https://www.lifefitness.com/en-eu/catalog/strength-training/plate-loaded/life-fitness-decline-chest-press) supports the seated pressing path and converging arms.
- [Hammer Strength Iso-Lateral Low Row](https://www.lifefitness.com/en-us/catalog/strength-training/plate-loaded/plate-loaded-iso-lateral-low-row) supports the chest-supported low pull, independent arms, and back classification.
- [Hammer Strength Pendulum-X Squat](https://www.lifefitness.com/en-us/catalog/strength-training/plate-loaded) lists the machine among plate-loaded lower-body equipment; its manufacturer comparison guide describes the guided pendulum squat design.

The bilingual cues are original editorial summaries and include setup, execution, controlled return, and machine-safety instructions where relevant. New artwork is dedicated under CC0 1.0.

## Prompt set

Shared visual direction for each separate generation: “Use case: scientific-educational. Create ONE ORIGINAL square two-phase exercise illustration. The attached `assisted-pull-up.webp` is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Match its high-detail, high-contrast grayscale anatomical athlete, black shorts/shoes, selective vivid red target-muscle highlight, crisp true transparent cutout, large full figures, and two equal side-by-side START/FINISH panels. Same athlete and fixed camera in both panels. Show all relevant equipment and full body. Use a small red movement arrow. No text, labels, border, watermark, extra limbs, cropped equipment, or copied pull-up pose.” Each prompt added the following movement-specific positions and constraints:

- **Pallof Press:** Band fixed to a post at chest height to the athlete's side; hands at sternum, then pressed straight forward while the torso resists rotation. Red obliques.
- **Front-Foot-Elevated Split Squat:** Lead foot flat on a low step; high split stance, then lower the hips and bend both knees. Red lead quadriceps and glute.
- **Renegade Row:** High plank gripping two hex dumbbells; one dumbbell remains planted while the other rows toward lower ribs. Red rowing-side lat and upper back.
- **Single-Leg Box Squat:** One planted foot, other leg extended off the floor; stand, then lower to lightly touch a box. Red working quadriceps and glute.
- **Curtsy Lunge:** Stand, then step one leg diagonally behind the planted leg and lower with control. Red forward-side glute.
- **Prone Snow Angel:** Lie face-down; sweep straight arms from hips outward toward a Y beside the head. Red rear shoulders and upper back.
- **Kettlebell Halo:** Hold one kettlebell by its horns beside one shoulder, then move it around the back of the head toward the other shoulder. Red shoulders and upper traps.
- **Skater Squat:** Stand on one foot with the other bent behind; lower on the planted leg while the free knee approaches the floor. Red working quadriceps and glute.
- **Lateral Step-Down:** One foot planted on a low box; lower until the other heel lightly reaches the floor at its side. Red working quadriceps and glute.
- **Tate Press:** Lie on a flat bench with two dumbbells; start with bent elbows and weights lowered toward upper chest, then extend elbows above mid-chest. Red triceps.
- **Reverse Plank Leg Lift:** Hold a reverse plank on hands and heels; lift one straight leg while keeping hips raised. Red glutes and hamstrings.
- **Side Plank Reach-Through:** Hold a forearm side plank; sweep the upper arm from vertical beneath the torso while hips stay lifted. Red supporting-side obliques.
- **Bear Plank Shoulder Tap:** Hover both knees in a bear plank; lift one hand to touch the opposite shoulder while balancing on the other hand and both toes. Red abdominals and obliques.
- **Reverse Lunge to Knee Drive:** Begin in a reverse lunge; rise on the forward planted leg as the rear knee travels to hip height. Red planted-side quadriceps and glute.
- **Half-Kneeling Band Chest Press:** Anchor a resistance band behind at chest height; press both hands forward from the chest in a half-kneeling stance. Red pectorals.
- **Side Plank Star:** Hold a forearm side plank with feet stacked; lift the top straight leg while keeping hips high. Red supporting-side obliques and upper hip abductors.
- **Prone W Raise:** Lie face-down with elbows bent in a W; raise/rotate both hands and draw shoulder blades together without arching the torso. Red rear shoulders and upper back.
- **Standing Dumbbell Reverse Curl:** Stand with palms facing backward and two dumbbells by the thighs; curl to chest height while keeping elbows close. Red forearms and brachioradialis.
- **Dumbbell Kickstand Romanian Deadlift:** Offset stance with most weight on the front foot, rear toes as support; hinge at the hips with two dumbbells and return to standing. Red front-leg hamstrings and glute.
- **Standing Cable Hip Flexion:** Cable ankle cuff on one leg, stand beside a low pulley; draw the cuffed knee forward and upward without leaning back. Red front hip flexors and upper thigh.
- **Standing Bent-Knee Calf Raise:** Stand with knees softly bent and hands on a support; rise onto the balls of both feet while keeping knee bend. Red calves.
- **Bird-Dog Dumbbell Row:** From three-point quadruped support with one leg extended, row one dumbbell toward the ribs while keeping the torso steady. Red rowing-side lat.
- **Dumbbell Push Press:** Dip both knees slightly with dumbbells at the shoulders, then drive upward through the legs and press both weights overhead. Red quadriceps and shoulders.
- **Bodyweight Box Jump:** From a balanced floor stance, dip and swing the arms, then jump to land both feet fully on a stable low box. Red quadriceps and glutes.
- **Standing Band Shoulder External Rotation:** Band anchored across the body at elbow height; keep one bent elbow at the ribs while rotating that forearm outward. Red working shoulder.
- **Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Dumbbell Press:** Stay tall in a half-kneeling stance with one dumbbell at the shoulder, then press that arm overhead. Red working shoulder.
- **Medicine Ball Overhead Slam:** Hold one medicine ball overhead, then drive it downward with both hands while hinging and bending the knees. Red lats and abdominal region.
- **Dumbbell Lateral Lunge:** Hold two dumbbells by the sides, step wide laterally, bend the stepping knee and push the hips back while the other leg stays straight. Red bent-leg quadriceps and glute.
- **Standing Band Good Morning:** Stand on a loop band with it across the shoulders; hinge at the hips, then stand tall. Red hamstrings and glutes.
- **Bodyweight Jumping Jack:** Start with feet together and arms down; jump feet outward while raising both arms overhead. Red shoulders and calves.
- **Bodyweight Plank Jack:** Hold a straight-arm plank with feet together, then jump feet apart while hands and trunk stay planted. Red abdominal and outer hip muscles.
- **Dumbbell Thruster:** Start in a deep squat with two dumbbells at the shoulders, then stand and press both weights overhead in one movement. Red quadriceps, glutes and shoulders.
- **Bodyweight Mountain Climber:** Begin in a straight-arm high plank, then draw one knee forward beneath the torso without moving the planted hands. Red abdominals and hip flexors.
- **Kettlebell Windmill:** Keep one kettlebell vertically overhead while hinging sideways at the hips and reaching the free hand toward the opposite shin. Red obliques and hip muscles.
- **Bodyweight Inchworm:** Hinge to place both hands on the floor near the feet, then walk the hands forward to a long high plank. Red abdominals and shoulders.
- **Bodyweight Squat Jump:** From a deep squat, drive vertically into a jump with both feet clear of the floor. Red quadriceps, glutes and calves.

- **Two-Dumbbell Hang Clean:** Hinge with two dumbbells near the thighs, then extend the hips and bring the weights to a front rack at shoulder height. Red glutes, quadriceps and upper traps.
- **World's Greatest Stretch:** In a long low lunge, reach one elbow toward the inside of the lead foot, then rotate the chest and raise that arm. Red hip and upper-back regions.
- **Bodyweight Squat Thrust:** Begin in a low squat with hands on the floor, then jump the feet backward into a long high plank. Red shoulders, abdominals and quadriceps.

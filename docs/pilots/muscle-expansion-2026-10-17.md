## Artwork provenance

Fourteen illustrations were already-created original pilots from the 2026-10-16 missing-movement set. Their WebP files were copied byte-for-byte into `media/exercises/`; the earlier pilot index documents their shared style direction and movement-specific prompt descriptions. Five additional illustrations were generated with OpenAI's built-in ImageGen tool, using the user's `dumbbell-bench-press.webp` reference and the Atlas `assisted-pull-up.webp` as style references only. They were visually checked before conversion to transparent 1024 × 1024 WebP at quality 85. No reference artwork was copied into the new assets.

The five additional prompt briefs were:

- **High-Cable Double-Biceps Curl:** two equal start/finish panels of a standing athlete between high pulleys, elbows open then flexed, biceps highlighted red.
- **Plate Pinch Carry:** two equal panels showing a controlled walk with pinched weight plates, forearms highlighted red.
- **Prone Reverse Hyperextension:** an athlete supported face down on a flat bench, legs raised only to torso level, lower-back target highlighted red.
- **Quadruped Adductor Rockback:** hands-and-knees position with one leg extended sideways, gentle hip rock, inner thigh highlighted red.
- **Seated Neck CARs:** seated neutral posture with a small controlled head rotation arc, neck highlighted red; avoid forced end range.

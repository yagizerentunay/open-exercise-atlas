## Artwork provenance

Six original exercise-specific images were generated with OpenAI built-in ImageGen using Atlas assisted-pull-up.webp and the approved Dumbbell Floor Press pilot as style references only: Chest Dip, Pec Deck Fly, Pendlay Row, JM Press, Toes-to-Bar, and Landmine Rotation. Outputs were resized to 1024 × 1024 and converted to WebP at quality 85; artwork was not retouched.

The remaining three images—Single-Dumbbell Suitcase March, Medicine Ball Overhead Slam, and Bodyweight Jumping Jack—were copied byte-for-byte from the existing original pilot set. Their prompts are documented in the pilot index.

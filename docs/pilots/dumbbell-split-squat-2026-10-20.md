# Dumbbell Split Squat pilot — 20 October 2026

The new record is a stationary split squat with both feet on the floor. It is distinct from `dumbbell-bulgarian-split-squat`, whose rear foot rests on a bench. The record and illustration are general exercise references, not individualized coaching.

## Sources and original work

- [ACE stationary split squat progression](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/blog/5032/try-this-fat-burning-leg-workout/) explicitly describes a stationary split squat on the ground before rear-foot elevation.
- [ACE lunge mechanics](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/pros/expert-articles/6834/5-moves-for-a-better-butt/) supports the split stance, front-foot push and rear-foot balance cues. Atlas wrote its English and Turkish instructions anew.

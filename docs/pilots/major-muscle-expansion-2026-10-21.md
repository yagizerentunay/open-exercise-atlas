## Movement coverage and distinction

- **Chest:** Low-to-High Cable Fly and High-to-Low Cable Fly document opposite cable paths; Smith Machine Incline Bench Press adds a guided incline press distinct from the plate-loaded incline press; Close-Grip Machine Chest Press provides the close-handle triceps-focused push.
- **Back:** Chest-Supported T-Bar Row adds an angled chest-supported machine row; existing T-Bar Row uses an unsupported landmine setup. Single-Arm Iso-Lateral Machine Row adds independent unilateral plate-loaded pulling. Plate-Loaded Iso-Lateral Incline Press documents independent incline press arms; Plate-Loaded Iso-Lateral Wide Pulldown adds a distinct wide, back-and-out lever path; Machine Kneeling Leg Curl adds the supported kneeling curl station.
- **Forearms:** Barbell Reverse Wrist Curl adds direct wrist extension alongside the existing flexion, dumbbell-extension, and cable movements.
- **Triceps:** Cable Triceps Kickback adds a low-pulley elbow-extension path distinct from the dumbbell kickback and cable pushdowns.
- **Legs:** Belt Squat adds hip-belt loading; Single-Leg Seated Leg Curl and Single-Leg Leg Extension add unilateral machine knee flexion and extension; Standing Machine Leg Curl adds the upright unilateral curl setup; Single-Leg Leg Press adds one-sided sled pressing.

## Priority-family coverage audit — 22 October 2026

The current 370-record Atlas was compared with Life Fitness G4 and Hammer Strength machine families and checked by target muscle/equipment in the exercise JSON:

- **Chest:** flat, incline, and decline machine presses; close-grip and Smith variants; assisted/plate-loaded dips; pec deck; both upward and downward cable fly paths.
- **Back:** assisted and cable vertical pulls, plate-loaded wide pulldown with its distinct outward path, machine high/low/seated rows, chest-supported T-bar row, unilateral iso-lateral row, and machine pullover.
- **Forearms:** cable and free-weight wrist flexion/extension, pronation/supination, radial deviation, finger curls, wrist roller, and grip holds. No additional commonly used selectorized forearm station appeared in the checked manufacturer catalog.
- **Triceps:** machine extension and dip, close-grip machine press, cable pushdown, overhead/cross-body extension, and kickback.
- **Legs:** machine squat and leg-press patterns (including unilateral press), knee extension, lying/seated/standing/kneeling curls, hip thrust/kickback and ab/adduction, plus seated/standing calf raises.

The remaining similar catalog items either already map to these movement families or change only brand/handle details without adding a distinct movement. No other high-use gap in these priority groups was found in this pass.

# Muscle reference illustrations

The explorer includes an anatomical reference image for all 19 muscle codes in the Atlas vocabulary. The gallery picks up files from `media/muscles/<code>.png` automatically. These images are a visual index for the muscle filter; they are not exercise demonstrations and do not indicate that a record has passed technique review.

## Provenance

- The first 17 images were created 2026-10-13 by Open Exercise Atlas with OpenAI image generation. One generated 5-by-4 contact sheet was split into individual transparent PNGs; the three unused panels are not included. `neck.png` and `hip-flexors.png` were generated separately on 2026-10-14 using the existing illustrations as style references, then cropped and resized to 196 × 333 transparent PNGs. No third-party image assets were imported.
- The art uses grayscale anatomical figures with red target-muscle emphasis. Some neighboring muscle groups are approximate representations; these are editorial navigation aids and must not be treated as clinical anatomy or expert validation.

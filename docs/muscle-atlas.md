## Provenance

- All 20 files were redrawn on 2026-11-05 as original grayscale anatomical illustrations with red muscle highlights, using OpenAI image generation. A consistent contact sheet supplied the main set; lower-back and abductors were regenerated separately to correct their target areas. The individual PNGs use a pale sage-gray background, portrait crops, and a consistent full-body scale. No third-party image assets were imported.
- The art uses grayscale anatomical figures with red target-muscle emphasis. Some neighboring muscle groups are approximate representations; these are navigation aids and must not be treated as clinical anatomy.
